Brand logos shown in the store of Raphael

Raphael shows each service of its store with the logo of the company behind it, only to tell the
services apart. The logos are in app/src/main/service-logos/, and where each one was taken from is
written in app/src/main/service-logos/sources.json. They are not licensed to Raphael and stay the
property of their owners.

Obsidian, Atlassian, Cloudflare, Sentry, Stripe, Zapier, PayPal, Webflow, Wix, Netlify, Neon, Prisma,
