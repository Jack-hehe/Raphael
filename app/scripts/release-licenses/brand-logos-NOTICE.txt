Brand logos shown in Raphael's store, services and tool steps

Raphael shows each service with the logo of the company behind it, only to tell the
services apart. The logos are in app/src/main/service-logos/, and where each one was taken from is
written in app/src/main/service-logos/sources.json. They are not licensed to Raphael and stay the
property of their owners.

Raphael is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of them. A logo is shown unaltered
except that a backdrop may be removed and, for a mark that is black in its official form, a white
version is shown on the dark theme. Tavily's dark-theme tile and arrow fills are inverted.

The existing Simple Icons paths are from 16.35.0. The added OpenRouter, Brave and SearXNG paths
are Simple Icons artwork, checked identical to the upstream files (develop branch) on 2026-10-23.
Upstream: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (icons/openrouter.svg,
